Ways to protect the margin
  • Get a chimney contractor quote during attorney review and negotiate the full amount off price.
  • Offer below ask — 130+ days, one fall-through and three cuts give leverage.
  • Use ductless mini-splits rather than ducting an 1850 house.
  • Keep finishes period-appropriate; buyers here are paying for character, not a gut.
  • Sewer-scope and moisture-check the stone foundation before removing contingencies.
