🏡 Delmar Flip Finder
Monday, November 2, 2026  ·  Fix-and-flip candidates in Delmar / Bethlehem (Bethlehem Central schools)
This week's read
Delmar is a tight, high-demand market in the Bethlehem Central School District — only 9 single-family listings under $400K this week, and most are already-updated homes, condos or land rather than true fixers. The real plays are dated-but-sound houses to modernize (1191 Delaware Turnpike, 21 Mccormack). One reality check drives the math below: a fully renovated 1,800 sqft home at 618 Kenwood has sat since July and been cut $50K to $309K — so in this district a $600K+ resale only pencils on larger or expanded footprints (2,500+ sqft); for the smaller fixers here, realistic renovated resale is closer to $375K–$540K. Two of the best leads — 598 Feura Bush (a true fixer) and the 506 Delaware two-family — went pending this week.

Summary. Owned by the same family for decades and "ready for its next chapter." $309,000  ·  3 bd / 2 ba / 1,800 sqft  ·  $172/sqft
Summary. Someone else's flip-in-progress: this 1880 colonial sold for $180,000 in Dec 2025, was renovated ("new kitchens, new baths, new siding and windows") and relisted at $359K in July — then cut five times, now $309K, with 24,000+ views and no buyer. As a comp, it's the single most useful data point this week: a finished 1,800 sqft Delmar home is stuck at $309K — a hard ceiling that sets realistic ARVs for the smaller fixers above.
